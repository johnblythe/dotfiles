# Meetings

Meetings are **expensive**. 1 hour × 6 people = 6 engineer-hours.

Default to async. Meet when: real-time discussion needed, relationship-building matters, async would take longer.

---

## Hygiene

**Before:** Agenda shared. Attendees actually required. Pre-read sent. Outcome stated.

**During:** Start on time. Facilitator. Note-taker. Tangents to parking lot. End with actions + owners.

**After:** Notes shared. Actions tracked. Recording posted.

---

## Cadences

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Standup | Daily | 15 min |
| Sprint Planning | Sprint start | 1-2 hrs |
| Refinement | Mid-sprint | 1 hr |
| Demo | Sprint end | 30-60 min |
| Retro | Sprint end | 1 hr |
| 1:1s | Weekly | 30 min |
| Tech Sync | Biweekly | 1 hr |

---

## Meeting Types

**Decision:** Come with options, leave with decision, document it.

**Brainstorm:** Diverge then converge. No "won't work" during divergence.

**Status:** Should probably be an email.

**Working session:** Actually doing work. 2-4 people max.

---

## Fail Modes

| Mode | Symptom | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| No agenda | "What are we here for?" | Cancel or demand one |
| Too many people | 10 invited, 2 talk | Reduce attendees, others read notes |
| Call addiction | "Let's hop on quick" for everything | Async first |
| Zombie recurring | Never canceled, rarely useful | Review cadences quarterly |
| Email as meeting | Could've been written | Send the email |
| Calendar Tetris | Back-to-back all day | Block focus time, batch meetings |
| No outcome | 1 hour of talk, nothing decided | State desired outcome upfront |
| Late start | "Waiting for stragglers" | Start on time, punish lateness not punctuality |
