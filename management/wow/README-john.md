# Working with John: the cheat sheet

Here's the deal: you'll figure most of this out on your own. But if a little preview helps, here's how I work and what I care about.

---

## The short version

I took a winding path to engineering (didn't fully commit until ~26). I like building things and building people. I'm curious about way too much stuff—but I try not to make it my whole personality.

I've got a family, so my schedule flexes. That said, I'm usually pretty available. Never hesitate to reach out.

---

## How I lead

Servant leadership meets player-coach. I like getting on the ice and working up a sweat alongside you. When I do, it's usually to help keep things moving toward the goal.

Context over control. You understanding what we're up against matters more than me playing dollar-store dictator. I'd rather you know the why than just follow orders.

---

## Communication

**Default:** Slack-first, async. DM me, drop it in a channel—no ceremony required.

**The principle:** Match the channel to the thinking.

- Quick questions, updates, "hey look at this" → Slack, real-time
- Complex decisions, strategy, feedback → Write it down, let it breathe, then talk

But honestly? Clarity beats channel every time. Don't overthink it.

---

## What I value

**Transparency.** Share early, share often. No hidden agendas.

**Curiosity.** Ask why. Dig deeper. Challenge assumptions—yes, including mine.

**Ownership.** See a problem? Own fixing it. Drive things to completion.

---

## What frustrates me

**Silence.** Not knowing where things stand. Stuck? Say so. Fine? Say that too.

**Agenda-less meetings.** If we're meeting, know why.

**Learned helplessness.** Google it first. Read the docs. Poke around. Then ask—I'm happy to help after you've tried.

---

## Feedback

I give it in real-time. Constructive feedback stays private; praise goes public.

And feedback goes both ways. I end most 1:1s with "Feedback for me?" because I mean it.

---

## 1:1s

Your meeting, your agenda.

I think of them as three legs of a stool:

- **Personal:** How are you? Life, energy, stress—whatever you're comfortable sharing.
- **Project:** What's happening right now? Blockers, wins, concerns.
- **Professional:** Longer-term. Where do you want to go? What do you want to learn?

We don't have to cover all three every time. Sometimes we just talk.

---

## Things people misread

**I think out loud.** My first take isn't final. I'm processing. Push back.

**I ask a lot of questions.** Curiosity, not interrogation. I'm trying to understand, not catch you.

**I go quiet when processing.** That's thinking, not frustration.

**I get weird.** Analogies, themes, quotes from random domains—philosophy, sports, music. If I say something obscure, ask me to translate.

---

## TL;DR

Talk to me—silence is worse than bad news. Own your stuff—ask for help after you've tried. Be curious—challenge me. 1:1s are yours—bring what matters. I adapt—tell me what you need.

Let's do this. 👋
