# Sprint Rhythm

Concrete calendar for 2-week sprints. For principles, see [sprint-mechanics.md](./sprint-mechanics.md).

All teams on same cadence: **Monday start, two Fridays later end.**

---

## Sprint Week 1

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| **Mon** | Sprint Planning → Goals locked | PM + EM + Lead |
| **Tue-Wed** | Execution | Lead monitors |
| **Thu-Fri** | Async planning poker board posted | PM + Lead |
| **Fri** | Week 1 close. Goal health check | Lead → EM |

---

## Sprint Week 2

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| **Mon** | Mid-sprint calibration (at risk? scope cut?) | Lead + EM |
| **Tue-Wed** | Execution. Team reviewing planning poker async | ICs |
| **Thu** | Refinement session (backlog ready, estimates discussed) | PM + Lead + EM |
| **Fri** | Demo → Retro → Sprint closes | EM (Lead supports) |

---

## Daily (Both Weeks)

| Activity | Owner | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Async standup posts | ICs | Goal-oriented, flex timing |
| Blocker scan + escalation | Lead | Aspirational: by midday |
| Jira hygiene pass | Lead | Status, points updated |

---

## Weekly (EM Layer)

| Activity | When | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| 1:1s | Throughout week | EM schedules |
| Lead sync | Once/week | Each lead, 15-30 min |
| Stakeholder blog | Fri or Mon | Lead helps prep |

---

## Key Handoffs

| From | To | What | When |
|------|----|------|------|
| PM + Lead | Team | Planning poker board | Thu/Fri Week 1 |
| Lead | EM | Goal health status | Fri Week 1 |
| Lead + EM | PM | At-risk items | Mon Week 2 |
| Team | Stakeholders | Demo | Fri Week 2 |
