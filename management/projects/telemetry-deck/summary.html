What the Comments Reveal

Clinic (82K) — "NON APPROVED SITE"

25K+ variations of the same message. Requests routed to sites that can't process them. A site approval lookup at routing time prevents these entirely.

Global Field Input (54K) — classification errors

"NOT A MEDICAL RECORD REQUEST" (3.4K), "LANGUAGE BARRIER" (914), "PATIENT NOT FOUND" (600+). Requests that should never have reached fulfillment.

Internal Department (61K) — 5 day median

Highest queue time of any reason. Even 30% reduction = ~250 FTE-equivalents reclaimed.
