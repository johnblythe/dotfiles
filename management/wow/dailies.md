# Dailies

Standups must be: **Quick. Relevant. Actionable.**

---

## Quick

15 minutes max. If longer, you're doing it wrong.

---

## Relevant

About the team more than the work. If it affects the sprint, it's relevant.

---

## Actionable

If you can't do anything with it, it shouldn't be said here.

**In:** People who have or need shared context.
**Out:** People with no overlap to current work.

## Fail Modes

| Mode | Symptom | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| Too big | Team >8, standup >15 min | Split into scrum teams |
| Info dump | One person talks 5+ min | "Parking lot, let's sync after" |
| Bikeshed | Debating implementation details | "Take it offline" |
| Social hour | Catching up on weekends | Move to happy hour or end of standup |
| Ghost updates | "Same as yesterday" for 3+ days | Check if stuck or work too large |
| Planning creep | Standup becomes refinement | Separate meetings exist for that |
| Wrong audience | BI engineer reporting to wrong team | Revisit who actually needs to be here |
