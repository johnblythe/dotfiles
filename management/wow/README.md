## Principles

1. **Reliability over speed** — Predictable beats heroic
2. **Written over verbal** — Warm/cold scales; hot doesn't
3. **Explicit over implicit** — Say the thing
4. **Boring over clever** — Proven > novel
5. **Outcomes over activity** — Ship > busy
