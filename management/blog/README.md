# Team Blog

Short, punchy updates on what's happening across the teams. Weekly or biweekly cadence.

## Purpose
- Visibility into team progress (good and bad)
- Celebrate wins
- Surface challenges honestly
- Keep stakeholders informed without meetings

## Format
- ~300-500 words
- 3-5 bullet highlights
- 1-2 challenges/blockers
- What's next
