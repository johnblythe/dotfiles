# TL Health Check — Mar 5, 2026

Context: TLs have been running ~6 weeks without you in standups/refinement. You're leaving in ~2.5 weeks. This conversation is about tightening what they've been doing and setting them up to run fully independently.

---

## Frame the Conversation

Open with:
- "You've been running without me in the room for 6 weeks. That was intentional. Now I want to hear what's working, what's not, and what needs to change before I'm fully gone."
- Be direct: you're leaving, timeline is ~2.5 weeks, they need to be self-sufficient

---

## Health Check Questions

### 1. Sprint Execution
- How are standups going? Still goal-oriented or drifting to status reports?
- Are blockers surfacing early or finding out late?
- Is the async standup format working, or does the team need more sync time?
- How's the Week 1 → Week 2 rhythm? (planning poker Thu/Fri W1, execution, refinement Thu W2, demo/retro Fri W2)

### 2. Decision Making
- What decisions are you making confidently vs. where do you hesitate?
- Are there things you've been deferring because I wasn't around to escalate to?
- How's the boundary between "lead decides" and "escalate to EM/PM"?

### 3. Sprint Hygiene
- Jira status accuracy — are tickets moving through states or piling up in "In Progress"?
- Story points filled on everything in sprint?
- Are goals being set at sprint start, and are they outcome-shaped (not task-shaped)?

### 4. Cross-Team / Dependencies
- Any dependency coordination gaps without me?
- How are you handling requests from other teams mid-sprint? (Goal drift defense)
- PR review bottlenecks? (segue into the PR hygiene work you've been doing)

### 5. Team Dynamics
- Anyone struggling or disengaged?
- Are there skill gaps you're working around?
- How's pairing/swarming going — happening organically or needs encouragement?

### 6. What's Missing
- What do you need from me in the next 2.5 weeks?
- What do you need from the new EM when they start?
- What process gaps would you fix if you could?

---

## Things to Listen For

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| "Standups are fine" (vague) | May be going through motions | Dig: what changed since I left? |
| "I'm not sure I should be deciding X" | Confidence gap, not competence gap | Explicitly expand their authority |
| "We haven't done retros" | Feedback loop broken | Re-establish before you leave |
| "Goals slip and we just carry over" | Sprint discipline eroding | Reinforce: scope cut > silent carry-over |
| "Other teams keep pulling us" | Goal drift, no shield | Help them practice saying no; give them the language |
| "Jira is messy" | Hygiene dropped | Run /hygiene together, make it a habit |

## What to Calibrate

### Authority Expansion
They've been leads for 6 weeks. Time to expand scope for the gap period:
- **Explicitly delegate**: retro facilitation, mid-sprint scope decisions, stakeholder comms
- **Define the escalation path without you**: who do they go to? (their skip? PM? new EM when they start?)
- **Give them permission to say no** to mid-sprint asks — in writing if needed

### Q2 Transition
- Share the Q2 planning docs (platform hypotheses, digital guidance) — they need to know what's coming
- If TL assignments might change for Q2, discuss now (don't surprise them)
- Ask: "Would you want to continue as lead in Q2?" — gives them agency

### Process Tightening
Based on what you hear, pick 1-2 things to tighten. Not everything. Options:
- [ ] Reinstate retros if they've dropped
- [ ] Tighten async standup format (template, timing, accountability)
- [ ] Add a weekly lead sync (15 min, just the TLs + you for now, then TLs + new EM)
- [ ] Run a hygiene pass together so they see the tool in action
