# Team Lead

A lead is **execution support**, not people leadership. They keep the sprint moving so the EM can focus on people and strategy.

---

## Core Responsibilities

### Sprint Execution
- **Run standups** — Keep them short, surface blockers, park tangents
- **Ticket management** — Help swap, rescope, or descope work as sprint evolves, anchored to sprint goal
- **Sprint hygiene** — Jira status updates, story points filled, tickets transitioned
- **Demo prep** — Help ensure team has demo material ready

### Escalation & Defense
- **Blocker first-responder** — First line for coordination/dependency issues; escalate to EM/PM when needed
- **Goal drift defense** — Push back on distractions or escalate to EM/PM; protect sprint commitments

### Planning Support
- **Pre-planning/refinement prep** — Work with EM and PM to get backlog ready
- **Estimation sanity check** — Gut-check estimates before planning for reasonableness

### Technical Guidance
- **IC decision support** — Guide lower-level decisions aligned to goals/strategy
- **PR review coordination** — Help reviews avoid bottleneck, route to right people
- **Pairing/swarming** — Encourage/organize ad-hoc sessions when work is stuck
- **Spec follow-through** — Ensure technical decisions get documented (ADRs, specs)

### Communication
- **Stakeholder blogging** — Help EM with weekly/biweekly updates
- **Run retros** — In EM's absence

---

## Not In Scope

These stay with the EM:

- Formal 1:1s
- Career development
- Performance feedback
- Conflict resolution
- Compensation/promotion
- People accountability

---

## Fail Modes

| Mode | Symptom | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| Hero | Does all hard work, team doesn't grow | Delegate, let others struggle productively |
| Bottleneck | All decisions flow through lead | Empower ICs to decide within guardrails |
| Absent | In meetings all day, team adrift | Block maker time, stay available |
| Over-shielding | Team unaware of context | Share enough for ownership |
| Conflict-avoidant | Issues fester | Surface to EM early |
